Lydia Oyawole, DNP(C), APRN, PMHNP-BC

Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

Lydia Oyawole is a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with extensive experience providing mental health care across inpatient, outpatient, and partial hospitalization settings. Her clinical background includes psychiatric assessment, medication management, crisis intervention, patient education, and collaborative treatment planning for individuals with a wide range of mental health needs.

Her approach to care is compassionate, individualized, and evidence-based, with an emphasis on understanding each patient beyond their symptoms. She values thoughtful assessment, open communication, and shared decision-making to develop treatment plans that reflect each patient's unique needs and goals.

Above all, Lydia strives to create a welcoming and supportive environment where patients and families feel heard, respected, and actively involved in their care.
