Notice of Privacy Practices

How medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to it.

What Is Protected Health Information?

Protected health information (PHI) is individually identifiable information about your past, present, or future physical or mental health, healthcare services, or payment for healthcare. PHI may be recorded electronically, on paper, or communicated orally. This Notice applies to PHI maintained by Cedar Bloom Wellness in connection with its outpatient psychiatric services.

Our Legal Responsibilities

Maintain the privacy and security of PHI as required by applicable law.

Provide this Notice describing our legal duties and privacy practices.

Notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured PHI when legally required.

Follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect.

Uses and Disclosures That Generally Do Not Require Written Authorization

Treatment

We may use and disclose PHI to evaluate and treat you, coordinate your psychiatric care, manage medications, communicate with pharmacies and other treating clinicians, arrange laboratory monitoring, and make referrals as permitted by law. More protective confidentiality laws may apply to certain records.

Payment

We may use and disclose PHI for eligibility checks, claims, billing, collection of payments, and other payment-related activities, subject to applicable law.

Healthcare Operations

We may use and disclose PHI for quality improvement, practice management, auditing, compliance, credentialing, and other lawful healthcare operations.

Other Circumstances Permitted or Required by Law

Certain disclosures may be made as required by law or for public health activities, reports of abuse or neglect, health oversight, appropriate judicial or administrative processes, specific law-enforcement purposes, workers’ compensation, authorized government functions, or to avert a serious and imminent threat to health or safety. We will follow applicable legal conditions and limits, including any more protective New Jersey requirements.

Uses and Disclosures That Require Authorization

Uses and disclosures not otherwise permitted or required by law generally require your written authorization. Most uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes, marketing uses requiring authorization, and sale of PHI require written authorization unless a legal exception applies. You may revoke an authorization in writing, except to the extent action has already been taken in reliance on it.

Information disclosed to an authorized recipient may be redisclosed by that recipient and may no longer be protected by HIPAA, although other laws may still apply.

Additional Protections for Psychiatric and Substance Use Disorder Information

Psychiatric records and information about minors may be subject to special federal and New Jersey confidentiality requirements. Cedar Bloom Wellness will apply those requirements where relevant. Parents or guardians do not automatically have access to every category of a minor’s confidential information; applicable law controls.

Psychotherapy notes, as specifically defined under HIPAA, receive additional protections distinct from ordinary progress notes and the clinical record.

If Cedar Bloom Wellness creates, receives, or maintains substance use disorder treatment records protected by 42 CFR Part 2, applicable Part 2 protections will be observed. Part 2 records or testimony describing them generally cannot be used or disclosed in civil, criminal, administrative, or legislative proceedings against a patient without the patient’s written consent or an appropriate court order and accompanying legal process, as required by law.

Your Rights Regarding Your PHI

1. Request restrictions

You may request in writing that we limit certain uses or disclosures for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations, or disclosures to individuals involved in your care. We are not generally required to agree. However, if you pay in full out of pocket for a healthcare item or service, we must honor a qualifying request not to disclose related PHI to a health plan for payment or healthcare operations unless disclosure is required by law.

2. Request confidential communications

You may ask us to contact you at an alternative address or through a particular telephone number or other reasonable means. We will accommodate reasonable requests.

3. Inspect or receive a copy

You may request access to or a paper/electronic copy of PHI in your designated record set, subject to lawful exceptions. We generally respond within 30 days; a permitted extension or reasonable cost-based fee may apply.

4. Request an amendment

You may request correction of PHI you believe is inaccurate or incomplete. We may deny the request under circumstances permitted by law and will explain a denial as required.

5. Receive an accounting of disclosures

You may request an accounting of certain disclosures made during the six years preceding your request, subject to exclusions under HIPAA. Fees may apply to additional requests within a 12-month period where permitted.

6. Receive a paper copy

You may obtain a paper copy of this Notice at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

7. Designate a representative

A legally authorized personal representative may exercise rights on your behalf, subject to verification and applicable protections for minors and other individuals.

8. File a complaint without retaliation

If you believe your privacy rights were violated, you may file a complaint with the Cedar Bloom Wellness Privacy Officer or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you.

Electronic Records, Patient Portal, and Telehealth

Cedar Bloom Wellness uses electronic systems, including AdvancedMD as configured by the practice, for scheduling, registration, documentation, patient communications, telehealth, and billing. We use safeguards appropriate to the information involved. Patients should use the designated secure patient portal for sensitive clinical communications rather than ordinary public website forms or unsecured messages.

Fundraising Communications

Cedar Bloom Wellness does not currently plan to use PHI for fundraising communications. If this practice changes, applicable notice and opt-out requirements will be followed.

Changes to This Notice

We may change this Notice and make the revised terms effective for PHI we already maintain and information received in the future, as permitted by law. The current Notice will be available on our website, at any applicable physical office, and upon request.
