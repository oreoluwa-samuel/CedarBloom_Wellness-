Before Your Visit

Patients should be in a private, quiet location with a reliable internet connection and functioning camera and microphone. Current medications and preferred pharmacy information should be readily available. Identity and current physical location may be confirmed at the beginning of the appointment. For minors, parent or legal guardian participation may be required when appropriate.

Telehealth Eligibility

Telehealth may not be appropriate for every patient or clinical situation. An in-person evaluation or higher level of care may be recommended when clinically necessary.

Emergency or crisis? Cedar Bloom Wellness is an outpatient psychiatric practice and does not provide emergency or crisis services. If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate or life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For mental health crisis support, call or text 988.
